## Step 2: Three recipes

### Recipe 1 — Ciorbă de pui cu lămâie (Romanian lemon chicken soup)
**Balkan comfort · 45 min · Serves 4 · Easy**

- **From the fridge:** chicken carcass plus 1 cup pulled meat, 2 potatoes (diced), 2 handfuls spinach, 1 lemon, 3 tbsp sour cream
- **Staples:** 1 onion, 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper
- **Optional:** 1 carrot, fresh lovage or dill
- **Steps:**
  1. Simmer the carcass in 1.5 L water with half the onion for 25 minutes. Strain and keep the broth.
  2. Soften the rest of the onion in oil, add the potatoes and broth, and cook 12 minutes.
  3. Add the pulled chicken and spinach for the last 3 minutes.
  4. Off the heat, whisk the sour cream with a ladle of hot soup, stir it back in, then sour with lemon juice to taste.
- **Zero-waste tip:** the carcass is the stock. Zest the lemon before you juice it and freeze the zest.
- **Swaps:** vegetarian: skip the chicken, use water and add a can of white beans. Gluten-free: as written. Dairy-free: leave out the sour cream and add extra lemon.
- **Allergens:** dairy.
- **Keeps:** 2 days in the fridge, because the chicken is already on day 2. Freezes well for 3 months if you freeze it before adding the sour cream.

### Recipe 2 — Lemony chicken & pea toasts
**Fast · 15 min · Serves 2 · Easy**

- **From the fridge:** 4 slices day-old bread, 1 cup pulled chicken, ½ cup frozen peas, 1 handful spinach, 2 tbsp sour cream, ½ lemon
- **Staples:** 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper
- **Steps:**
  1. Toast the bread in oil until golden and rub it with the cut garlic clove.
  2. Pour boiling water over the peas, drain, then mash with sour cream, lemon zest and juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Wilt the spinach in the same pan for 1 minute and add the chicken until hot all the way through.
  4. Spread the pea mash on the toasts and top with chicken and spinach.
- **Zero-waste tip:** day-old bread toasts crisper than fresh, so this is its best use.
- **Swaps:** vegetarian: replace the chicken with a fried egg. Gluten-free: use gluten-free bread. Dairy-free: mash the peas with olive oil instead of sour cream.
- **Allergens:** gluten, dairy.
- **Keeps:** eat right away. Assembled toasts do not keep and do not freeze.

### Recipe 3 — Spinach & potato "spanakopita" skillet with bread-crumb crust
**Greek-inspired · 35 min · Serves 3 · Medium**

- **From the fridge:** the last 2 handfuls spinach, 1 potato (thinly sliced), 3 tbsp sour cream, 1 cup frozen peas, 4 slices bread (blitzed or grated into crumbs), ½ lemon
- **Staples:** 1 onion, 2 tbsp oil, salt, pepper
- **Optional:** 1 egg to bind it
- **Steps:**
  1. Fry the onion and potato slices in an oven-safe pan for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the spinach and peas until wilted, then take the pan off the heat.
  3. Mix the sour cream with lemon zest and juice (and the egg, if using) and fold it through the vegetables.
  4. Top with the crumbs tossed in oil and bake at 200 °C / 400 °F for 12–15 minutes until golden.
- **Zero-waste tip:** bread heels make the best crumbs. Any extra crumbs go in the freezer for the next crust.
- **Swaps:** vegetarian: as written. Gluten-free: top with gluten-free crumbs or crushed potato chips. Dairy-free: replace the sour cream with 2 tbsp olive oil.
- **Allergens:** gluten, dairy, and egg if you add the optional egg.
- **Keeps:** 3 days in the fridge. Not a good freezer dish: the potato and sour cream turn watery.

**Cook order:** Recipe 1 tonight (it uses the carcass and half the chicken), Recipe 2 tomorrow for lunch (the rest of the chicken, still inside day 3), Recipe 3 tomorrow night or the day after (the last of the wilting spinach).
