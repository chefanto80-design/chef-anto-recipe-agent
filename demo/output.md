## Step 2: Three recipes

### Recipe 1 — Ciorbă de pui cu lămâie (Romanian lemon chicken soup)
**Balkan comfort · 45 min · Serves 4 · Easy**

- **From the fridge:** chicken (meat pulled, carcass kept), 2 potatoes (diced), a handful of spinach, 1 lemon, 2 tbsp sour cream
- **Staples:** 1 onion, 1 carrot *(optional)*, 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper
- **Optional:** fresh lovage or dill
- **Steps:**
  1. Simmer the chicken carcass in 1.5 L water with half the onion for 25 minutes. Strain.
  2. Sauté the rest of the onion in oil, add potatoes and the broth, cook 12 minutes.
  3. Add pulled chicken and spinach for the last 3 minutes.
  4. Off the heat, whisk sour cream with a ladle of hot soup, stir it back in, then sour with lemon juice to taste.
- **Zero-waste tip:** the carcass *is* the stock. Nothing from the bird goes in the bin.
- **Swaps:** dairy-free: skip sour cream, add an extra squeeze of lemon. Gluten-free as written.

### Recipe 2 — Lemony chicken & pea toasts
**Fast · 15 min · Serves 2 · Easy**

- **From the fridge:** 4 slices day-old bread, 1 cup pulled chicken, ½ cup peas (thawed), handful of spinach, 2 tbsp sour cream, ½ lemon
- **Staples:** 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper
- **Steps:**
  1. Toast bread in oil until golden; rub with the cut garlic clove.
  2. Mash peas with sour cream, lemon zest and juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Wilt spinach in the same pan for 1 minute, add chicken to warm through.
  4. Spread the pea mash on the toasts, top with chicken and spinach.
- **Zero-waste tip:** stale bread toasts better than fresh; this is the best use for it.
- **Swaps:** vegetarian: skip chicken, add a fried egg. Dairy-free: use olive oil instead of sour cream in the mash.

### Recipe 3 — Spinach & potato "spanakopita" skillet with bread-crumb crust
**Greek-inspired · 35 min · Serves 3 · Medium**

- **From the fridge:** remaining spinach, 1 potato (thinly sliced), remaining sour cream, 1 cup peas, the rest of the bread (blitzed into crumbs), ½ lemon
- **Staples:** 1 onion, 2 tbsp oil, 1 egg *(optional, binds it)*, salt, pepper
- **Steps:**
  1. Fry onion and potato slices in an oven-safe pan for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in spinach and peas until wilted; take off the heat.
  3. Mix sour cream, lemon zest and the egg, fold through the vegetables.
  4. Top with bread crumbs tossed in oil; bake at 200 °C / 400 °F for 12–15 minutes until golden.
- **Zero-waste tip:** blitz any bread heels into crumbs and freeze them for the next crust.
- **Swaps:** vegetarian as written. Gluten-free: top with crushed potato chips or GF crumbs.
