## Recipe 1: Ciorbă de porc cu legume, soured with yogurt and egg (Balkan comfort)
A Romanian sour soup in the rădăuțeană style: the yogurt and egg-yolk finish makes it silky and tangy.

**Time:** 40 min | **Serves:** 4 | **Difficulty:** Easy–medium

**From the fridge**
- All the leftover roast pork (~300 g), cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 egg yolks (save the 2 whites for Recipe 3)
- 150 g (⅔ cup) yogurt
- 1 stale pita, torn into croutons

**Staples:** 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp oil, salt, pepper

**Optional:** lemon juice or a splash of vinegar, fresh dill or lovage, bay leaf

**Steps**
1. Warm 1 tbsp oil in a pot. Soften the diced onion and carrots for 5 min.
2. Add 1.5 L (6 cups) water, a bay leaf if you have one, and salt. Simmer 15 min until the carrots are tender.
3. Add the zucchini and simmer 5 min.
4. Add the pork and simmer 5 min, until the soup is steaming hot all the way through (pork back to 165°F / 74°C).
5. Meanwhile, toss the torn pita with 1 tbsp oil and a little crushed garlic. Toast in a dry pan or at 400°F (200°C) for 6–8 min until crisp.
6. Whisk the yolks and yogurt in a bowl. Ladle in some hot broth, a little at a time, whisking. Take the pot off the heat and stir the mix in. Don't boil it again or it will curdle.
7. Season with salt, pepper and lemon or vinegar to taste. Finish with dill and serve with the pita croutons on top.

**Zero-waste tip:** Simmer the carrot peels and onion skins in the water for 10 min first, then strain, for a free stock. Any pork fat or juices in the container go into the pot too, because that's flavor.

**Swaps:** Vegetarian: use 1 can white beans instead of pork. Gluten-free: skip the croutons or use GF bread. Dairy-free: use unsweetened plain plant yogurt, or just egg yolks and lemon.

**Allergens:** egg, dairy, gluten (croutons)

**Keeps:** Eat by tomorrow, because the pork is already 3 days old (keep the croutons separate). Don't freeze it once the yogurt-egg finish is in. If you want to freeze some, take it out after step 4, cool it fast and freeze for up to 2 months.

## Recipe 2: Zucchini-carrot fritters with red pepper yogurt dip (Greek-Balkan mezze)
In the style of kolokithokeftedes, using the stale pita as crumbs and the egg whites from Recipe 1.

**Time:** 30 min | **Serves:** 3 (about 10 fritters) | **Difficulty:** Easy

**From the fridge**
- 1 zucchini, grated
- 1 carrot, grated
- 1 egg + the 2 leftover egg whites from Recipe 1
- 1 stale pita, blitzed or grated into crumbs (~½ cup)
- Dip: 150 g (⅔ cup) yogurt + 2 tbsp roasted red pepper spread

**Staples:** 2 tbsp flour, 1 garlic clove, ½ small onion (finely chopped), oil for frying, salt, pepper

**Optional:** dill, mint or parsley, lemon zest, a pinch of chili flakes

**Steps**
1. Toss the grated zucchini with a pinch of salt and leave 5 min. Squeeze it hard in a clean towel. This is the key to crisp fritters.
2. Mix in the carrot, onion, egg, whites, pita crumbs, flour, herbs, salt and pepper. It should hold together when you shape it, so add a spoon more crumbs or flour if it's wet.
3. Heat a thin layer of oil over medium heat. Fry spoonfuls, flattened, for 3 min per side until deep golden. Drain on paper.
4. For the dip, stir the yogurt, pepper spread and half a grated garlic clove together and season.

**Zero-waste tip:** Keep the green zucchini juice you squeezed out and stir it into the ciorbă or a soup. Leftover fritters make a great wrap or breakfast with a fried egg.

**Swaps:** Vegetarian: as written. Gluten-free: use GF breadcrumbs plus rice flour or chickpea flour. Dairy-free: use plant yogurt in the dip.

**Allergens:** egg, gluten, dairy (dip). Check your pepper spread label, as some contain sesame or nuts.

**Keeps:** Fritters keep 2 days in the fridge (re-crisp in a hot pan or oven) and freeze for up to 2 months. The dip keeps 3 days.

## Recipe 3: Eggs baked in red pepper sauce with crispy pita (fast, under 20 min)
Balkan breakfast meets Miami brunch, all in one pan.

**Time:** 18 min | **Serves:** 2 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**From the fridge**
- 3 eggs
- 4 tbsp roasted red pepper spread
- 1 carrot, coarsely grated
- 100 g (scant ½ cup) yogurt, to serve
- 1 stale pita, cut into wedges

**Staples:** ½ onion (sliced), 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper

**Optional:** paprika, chili flakes, parsley

**Steps**
1. Warm the oil in a lidded pan. Cook the onion and carrot for 4 min.
2. Add the garlic, pepper spread and about 100 ml (scant ½ cup) water. Simmer 3 min into a loose sauce and season.
3. Make 3 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–6 min on low until the whites are fully set.
4. While the eggs cook, toast the pita wedges in a dry pan or toaster until crisp.
5. Top with spoonfuls of yogurt, paprika and herbs. Scoop it up with the pita.

**Zero-waste tip:** Swirl a splash of water in the pepper jar to catch the last of the spread, and add it straight into the sauce.

**Swaps:** Vegetarian: as written. Gluten-free: serve with GF bread or polenta (mămăligă). Dairy-free: skip the yogurt or use plant yogurt.

**Allergens:** egg, dairy, gluten (pita). Check the pepper spread label for sesame or nuts.

**Keeps:** Best eaten straight away. Leftover sauce without eggs keeps 3 days. Don't freeze.
